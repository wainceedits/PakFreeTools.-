What is an Age Calculator?

An age calculator calculates the difference between your date of birth and today's date. It can show your age in years, months and days.

How to Calculate Your Age

Select your date of birth from the calendar above and click the Calculate My Age button.

The calculator automatically compares your birth date with today's date and displays your current age.
