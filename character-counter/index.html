Free Online Character Counter

PakFreeTools Character Counter is a free online tool for counting characters in your text. It also provides word, line, sentence and paragraph counts.

It can be useful when writing social media posts, articles, assignments, messages, titles and other content where character limits are important.

What Does the Character Counter Show?

The tool shows the total number of characters, characters without spaces, words, lines, sentences and paragraphs.

How to Use the Character Counter

  1. Type or paste your text into the text box.
  2. The statistics update automatically.
  3. Check the total characters and characters without spaces.
  4. Use the other statistics to understand the structure of your text.

Why Count Characters?

Some websites and applications limit the number of characters that can be entered. A character counter makes it easier to check your text before submitting or publishing it.
