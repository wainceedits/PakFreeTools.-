Free Online Word Counter

PakFreeTools Word Counter is a free online tool that helps you count words and characters quickly. Simply type or paste your text into the box and the results will update automatically.

This tool can be useful for students, writers, bloggers, social media users and anyone who needs to check the length of their text.

What Does This Word Counter Count?

The tool provides several useful statistics including total words, characters, characters without spaces, sentences, paragraphs and estimated reading time.

How to Use the Word Counter

  1. Type or paste your text into the text box.
  2. The word count updates automatically.
  3. Check characters, sentences and paragraphs.
  4. Use the estimated reading time to understand how long the text may take to read.

Is This Word Counter Free?

Yes. The PakFreeTools Word Counter is free to use and does not require registration or an account.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the word counter work?

It analyzes the text you enter and calculates the number of words, characters, sentences and paragraphs directly in your browser.

Yes. The tool is designed to work on smartphones, tablets and desktop computers.
