What is a Percentage Calculator?

A percentage calculator helps you quickly find a percentage of a number. It is useful for calculating discounts, marks, profits, increases, decreases and many everyday calculations.

How to Calculate a Percentage

Enter the number and the percentage in the calculator above, then click the Calculate Percentage button.

The basic formula is:

Percentage value = Number × Percentage ÷ 100

For example, 20% of 500 is 100.
