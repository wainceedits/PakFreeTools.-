Free Online Case Converter

PakFreeTools Case Converter is a free online text formatting tool that lets you quickly change text between different letter cases.

You can convert text to uppercase, lowercase, title case, sentence case or capitalize individual words without manually editing every letter.

Available Text Cases

UPPERCASE

Converts all letters to capital letters.

lowercase

Converts all letters to lowercase letters.

Title Case

Capitalizes the first letter of each word.

Sentence Case

Makes the first letter of each sentence uppercase and the remaining letters lowercase.

Capitalize Words

Capitalizes the first letter of every word while keeping the remaining letters lowercase.

How to Use the Case Converter

  1. Type or paste your text into the text box.
  2. Select the case you want.
  3. Your text will be converted instantly.
  4. Use Copy Text to copy the converted result.
